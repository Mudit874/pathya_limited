# PATHYA

### Personalized Nutrition • Healthy Meals • Fitness • Wellness

**Pathya** is a personalized nutrition and wellness platform designed to help individuals build healthier lifestyles through customized diet plans, wholesome meals, fitness guidance, and lifestyle-focused services.

> **Pathya** means *"wholesome"* in Sanskrit — representing a way of life rooted in health, nutrition, and well-being.

**Live Website:** [https://pathya.vercel.app/](https://pathya.vercel.app/)

---

## About Pathya

Pathya goes beyond conventional meal delivery by combining **personalized nutrition planning, healthy food, fitness guidance, and wellness** into one experience.

The platform is designed around the idea that nutrition should adapt to an individual's lifestyle rather than forcing everyone into the same plan.

### Our Focus

* Personalized nutrition
* Healthy meal delivery
* Fitness and lifestyle
* Holistic wellness
* Calorie-conscious eating
* Ayurvedic wellness
* 1:1 fitness coaching

## Key Features

### Personalized Nutrition

Pathya helps users create nutrition plans according to their individual goals, body measurements, dietary preferences, and lifestyle.

Users can select goals such as:

* Weight Loss
* Healthy Eating
* Muscle Gain
* Diabetes Management

### BMI Calculator

The nutrition journey includes a BMI calculation step using:

* Height
* Weight
* Age

### Diet Preferences

Users can choose from different dietary approaches:

* Ketogenic
* Low Carb
* Balanced
* Vegan

### Meal Preferences

Pathya supports multiple meal preferences:

* Vegetarian
* Non-Vegetarian
* Eggetarian

### Personalized Meal Plans

The platform provides multiple meal-plan options with different durations and meal quantities.

| Plan         | Duration | Meals |    Price |
| ------------ | -------: | ----: | -------: |
| Trial        |   3 days |    12 |   ₹1,500 |
| Most Popular |  14 days |    56 |   ₹6,664 |
| Best Deal    |  30 days |   120 |  ₹13,200 |
| Custom       | Flexible |   20+ | Variable |

Meal plans can include:

* Breakfast
* Lunch
* Snacks
* Dinner

* Experience level
* Current weight

* Workout frequency
* Preferred workout time
* Physical limitations
* Motivation
